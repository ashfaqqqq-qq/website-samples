ABOUT ALEEZ

A fragrance that's

Aleez Perfumes is a small, independent fragrance studio based in Chennai. We didn't want to be another catalogue of ready-made bottles — so we built a studio where you describe the scent, and we make it.

Every order is made to order, from a fragrance you already love or a feeling you're trying to put into words. No mass production, no filler notes — just a scent that's yours.
